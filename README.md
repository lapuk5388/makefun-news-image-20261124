Entry for the [news-image-20261124 bounty on make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124):
one front page summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026 — 34 dated stories
across 9 regions, led by Huang on Nvidia, the Merkury story, and
Sparta II.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024): masthead THE MORNING STANDARD, dated story
cards carrying their sources, and a date check of the stories that
are that day's news. Every story is dated 24 November 2026 and drawn
from the day's locked pool, with the nine regions grouped so a reader can scan the whole world at a glance.

## How it is built

- `main.py` — the submitted entrypoint (standard library only). It
  opens `entry.png` beside it, checks the 1024×1024 and 1 MiB gates,
  and writes the image bytes exactly as its output, so both runs
  produce byte-identical bytes.
